About Me

Bridging research and production AI

I'm an AI Systems Architect and final-year Ph.D. candidate at the University of Miyazaki, Japan, specializing in Multimodal Computer Vision, LLM Systems, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation. I have a proven track record of bridging cutting-edge scientific research with robust, enterprise-grade software engineering.

My expertise spans GPU-accelerated model training, custom object tracking (PTA), fine-tuning pretrained CNNs with ArcFace, frame-mix false-positive reduction, custom OCR architectures (NRTR/CRNN), and TensorRT/ONNX optimizations — alongside architecting asynchronous microservices with Python, FastAPI, Qdrant, PostgreSQL, PyTorch, and Docker.

I'm an extensively published author in top-tier international journals with impact factors up to 4.9, including a first-author paper in Nature Portfolio's Scientific Reports.
